MISSION ICARUS-RLV

An autonomous Balloon-Launched Reentry Vehicle (BLARV) powered by deterministic C++/FreeRTOS multi-tasking on dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers[cite: 1]. Featuring active aerodynamic grid fin control, Kaltgas-RCS attitude adjustment, and Return-To-Launch-Site (RTLS) capabilities[cite: 1]. Payloads include radiation profiling at the Pfotzer-Maximum and dry yeast astrobiology exposure[cite: 1].

PROJEKT JOANUS

An autonomous Python data science pipeline designed to identify, extract, and validate exoplanet candidates from NASA space telescope archives (TESS, Kepler)[cite: 1]. Utilizing a Relativ-First metric, Box Least Squares (BLS) periodograms, and a robust False-Positive validation suite (Odd/Even transit depth checks)[cite: 1].
