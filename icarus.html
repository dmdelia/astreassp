AVIONICS & GNC

Built on a strict FreeRTOS multitasking architecture, decoupling mission-critical telemetry, sensor fusion, and active aerodynamic control loops.

Flight Computer

  • ESP32 Microcontroller: Dual-core processing with strict task prioritization.
  • Sensor Fusion: High-frequency 9-DOF IMU filtering combined with GPS integration for precise spatial orientation.
  • LoRa Telemetry: 868 MHz live downlink for real-time tracking and state-machine verification.

Active Control Systems

  • Cold-Gas RCS: Solenoid-actuated thrusters for anti-spin and attitude stabilization in near-vacuum environments (35,000m+).
  • Grid Fin Steering: 4x active servo-actuated aerodynamic grid fins enabling Return-To-Launch-Site (RTLS) precision gliding through the troposphere.

SCIENTIFIC PAYLOADS

Operating in the stratosphere demands hardware-in-the-loop validation for both physical phenomena and biological exposure.

  • Radiation Profiling: Interrupt-based Geiger-Müller counter capturing cosmic secondary radiation profiles precisely at the Pfotzer-Maximum (15–20 km altitude).
  • Astrobiology Stress Test: A custom servo-actuated sterile chamber exposing dry yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) to UV-C radiation, extreme cold, and near-vacuum, verified post-flight via CO₂ fermentation analysis.
  • Decoupled Optics: Standalone 4K camera payload capturing the Earth's curvature and deep space, triggered dynamically via hardware GPIO handshake pulses from the On-Board Computer.
↓ Flight Manifest

FLIGHT MANIFEST

Iterative, staged deployment minimizes risk while validating hardware from the ground up.

  • Phase 1 (M-1 & M-2): Low-altitude multicopter drop tests (300-500m) validating mechanical cutdowns, parachute state-machines, and active Grid Fin PID control for precision landing.
  • Phase 2 (M-3 & M-4): 1,600g stratospheric envelope flights (~35,000m). Full scientific deployment targeting the Pfotzer radiation curve and astrobiological yeast exposure.
  • Phase 3 (M-5 Titan Pinnacle): 3,000g flagship envelope (>40,000m). Near-space operations validating vacuum Cold-Gas RCS stabilization and full-profile RTLS reentry.
