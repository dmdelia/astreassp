Mission ICARUS-RLV

An autonomous Balloon-Launched Reentry Vehicle (BLARV) powered by deterministic C++/FreeRTOS multi-tasking on dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers.

Avionics & GNC

  • Flight Computer: ESP32 running FreeRTOS with strict task prioritization.
  • Attitude Control: Cold-Gas RCS for anti-spin and vacuum stabilization at 40,000m.
  • Aerodynamic Steering: 4x Active Servo Grid Fins for Return-To-Launch-Site (RTLS) precision landing.

Scientific Payloads

  • Radiation Profiling: Interrupt-based Geiger-Müller counter targeting the Pfotzer-Maximum.
  • Astrobiology Stresstest: Servo-actuated sterile chamber exposing dry yeast to UV-C and near-vacuum.
  • Imagery: Standalone 4K stratospheric camera triggered via GPIO.
