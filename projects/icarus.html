PROJECT 01 / FLIGHT SYSTEMS

ICARUS-RLV

Autonomous stratospheric reentry vehicle hardware testbed.

PROGRAM STATUSDEVELOPMENT
01 / OBJECTIVE

Testing the complete flight loop.

ICARUS-RLV is designed as a hardware and flight-software platform for testing autonomous guidance, navigation and control. The vehicle architecture brings together embedded computation, attitude control, active aerodynamic surfaces and recovery.

02 / SYSTEM
COMPUTE

ESP32

Deterministic C++ / FreeRTOS multitasking architecture on dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers.

GNC

RCS + GRID FINS

Cold-gas attitude control and active aerodynamic control surfaces for flight-state testing.

RECOVERY

RTLS

Return-to-launch-site architecture with parachute and landing-system validation.

03 / SCIENCE PAYLOAD
01

Radiation

Interrupt-based Geiger-Müller counting for cosmic secondary radiation profiling around the Pfotzer Maximum.

02

Astrobiology

Servo-actuated biological sample chamber for stratospheric UV-C, low-temperature and low-pressure exposure.

03

Optical

Standalone 4K camera payload triggered by hardware GPIO handshake from the onboard computer.
