JOANUS

Exoplanet discovery and validation pipeline built around relative photometry, BLS searches and automated false-positive checks.

PIPELINE / 01

Search first.
Validate independently.

JOANUS processes public space-telescope photometry with a relative-first approach, searches for periodic transit signatures and exports structured candidate telemetry for further validation.
